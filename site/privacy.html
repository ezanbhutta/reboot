Privacy

We collect what an order needs. Nothing else.

The short version: we collect what's needed to take payment, ship your order and answer your questions. We don't sell your data. You can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • When you order: name, delivery and billing address, email, phone number if you give one, and order contents. Card details go straight to our payment processor. We never see or store them.
  • When you contact us: your message and contact details, so we can reply and keep a record.
  • When you subscribe: your email address, until you unsubscribe.
  • When you browse: standard analytics covering pages viewed, roughly where you are, and what device you used.

Why

To take payment, deliver your order, handle returns and warranty claims, answer questions, meet our legal and tax obligations, and understand which parts of the site are and aren't working.

Who else sees it

Our payment processor, our delivery carrier, our email provider and our analytics provider. Each one gets only what they need to do their part. We do not sell your data or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep your cart and session working. Analytics and advertising cookies are optional and only set if you accept them. You can change your mind at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us and we'll action it within one month.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support messages for two years. Newsletter details until you unsubscribe.
