What's covered

Covered for two years: motor and pump failure, battery failure, charging faults, leaks from a manufacturing defect, and electronic faults.

Not covered: accidental damage, drops, wear to nozzles (a consumable), damage from descaling agents or anything other than water, and units opened or modified.

What happens next

Step 1
We reply within one working day, usually with a question or two.
Step 2
If it's a covered fault, we send a replacement. No return required in most cases.
Step 3
If we do need the unit back, we send a prepaid label.

Weak pressure is more often a blocked nozzle or a part-seated tank than a fault. Worth a quick check before you claim — but if in doubt, submit and we'll work it out together.
