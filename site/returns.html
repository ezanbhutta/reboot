Policies

Returns

Thirty days. We pay the postage.

The short version: thirty days from delivery, we pay return postage, and you get a full refund. Used is fine. That is the whole point.

The window

Thirty days from the day your order is delivered. Tell us within that window and we'll start the return, even if the parcel takes a few days to reach us afterwards.

Using it doesn't void the return

We'd rather you used it properly for a few weeks and made a real decision. Give it at least two weeks. Gum sensitivity settles in that time, and technique takes a few days to get right. A return on a used unit is completely fine.

How to return

Message us with your order number and we'll email a prepaid label. Pack it in the original box if you still have it; any secure box is fine if not.

Your refund

We refund to the original payment method within three working days of the parcel arriving. You'll get an email when it's processed. Depending on your bank it may take a few more days to appear.

Faulty rather than unwanted

If it's a fault rather than a change of mind, use the warranty claim instead. It is faster and usually doesn't require sending anything back.

Your legal rights

This policy sits alongside your statutory rights and does not replace them.
