Release 001

We started with the boring one.

A cordless water flosser. Not the product that makes a brand look exciting, but the one where the gap between what is sold and what actually works was widest.

Nearly everyone who gives up on a water flosser was using it wrong, and nobody had told them how.

So the product page is mostly technique. Where to aim it, which mode to start on, and what the first fortnight actually looks like. The hardware was the easy half.

How we choose

Four tests before anything gets a release number.

Is it a habit, or a novelty?

We only make things people will still be using in a year. If the honest answer is that it gets opened once and photographed, it does not get made.

Is the gap real?

There has to be something wrong with the category as it exists. With the flosser it was that most people are never taught how to use one, and quit inside a month.

Can we say what it is not?

Every product ships with a written account of what it will not do for you. If we cannot write that page honestly, we do not understand the product well enough to sell it.

Can we stand behind it for two years?

Two year warranty, thirty days to change your mind, and a claim handled through a form rather than a phone queue. If we are not confident enough to offer that, it is not ready.
