About

We started with the part everyone skips.

reboot is new. Here is what we are, and what we are not.

Most people are told to floss, feel guilty about not flossing, buy something that promises to fix it, and stop using that too. The oral care aisle is full of devices bought with good intentions and abandoned within a month.

We think the reason is simple and almost never addressed: the products are sold on a promise they cannot keep, and nobody explains how to use them properly.

What the name means

A reboot is not a reinvention. It is starting the same thing again, properly. We are not claiming to have invented a new category or solved oral health. We have made a well-built cordless water flosser and put real effort into the part everyone else treats as an afterthought. That means teaching you to use it so that it actually works.

What we will not do

  • Claim this replaces string floss. It doesn't, and we say so on every page where it matters.
  • Publish reviews we haven't earned, or press coverage we haven't received.
  • Print specifications we haven't verified against the production unit. Where a figure is outstanding, the site says "to be confirmed" rather than guessing.
  • Make medical claims. We can tell you what the device does mechanically. What that means for your gums is a conversation for your dentist.
  • Use countdown timers, invented stock levels, or fake urgency of any kind.

Where we are

This is our first product. We have no trading history and no reviews yet, and we would rather earn those than manufacture them. In the meantime, what we can offer is a two-year warranty, thirty days to change your mind with return postage paid, and support that answers within a working day.

If that isn't enough to take a chance on an unknown brand, we understand. Come back when there are reviews.
