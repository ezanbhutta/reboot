Technique

Three things decide whether this works for you.

Not pressure. Aim.

Step 01

Start on Soft

Lukewarm water, and press the mode button until you are on Soft. Build up over the first week rather than starting at maximum.

Step 02

Aim where the tooth meets the gum

Hold the nozzle at roughly ninety degrees and trace the line where the tooth meets the gum. Pause briefly at each one.

Step 03

Lean over the sink, lips nearly closed

Let the water fall out as you go. Forty seconds, no mess.

Control

Four modes, and the one to start on.

The mode button steps through them. Pressure across the four runs from 40 to 120 PSI, pulsing 1600 times a minute.

40 120 PSI

Soft

Where to start, and where to stay while your gums settle. Also the one to use if anything feels tender.

Standard

Daily cleaning once the first fortnight is behind you. Most people end up here and stop thinking about it.

High

The most pressure the pump gives. Useful around brackets and bridges, unnecessary everywhere else.

Pulse

Alternates strong and weak along the gumline rather than holding one pressure. It is a massage setting, not a cleaning one.

The nozzle drops into a sealed collar and turns a full circle in it. You point the tip where it needs to go and rotate the handle, rather than rotating your wrist.

Four indicator dots show the mode. The fifth light is the battery: green while running, red on charge, green again when it is full. It stops on its own at ninety seconds.

Questions

Before you decide.

Will I see bits of food come out?

Sometimes, sometimes not. That is not a sign it is failing. Most of what it removes is soft plaque at the gumline, which you will not see. Judge it by how your gums look after two weeks.

Can I stop using string floss?

We would rather you did not. They do different jobs. If you genuinely will not floss, this is far better than nothing, but it is not a straight swap.

My gums bleed. Should I stop?

Usually not. It is generally existing inflammation rather than damage, and it typically settles within one to two weeks. Start low. If it has not improved after a fortnight, speak to your dentist.

Is it messy?

Only if you stand upright with your mouth open. Lean over the sink, lips almost closed. Everyone gets it within a couple of uses.

What if I do not get on with it?

Thirty days from delivery, we pay return postage, and you get a full refund. Use it properly for a few weeks first. That is what the window is for.

Who should not use one?

Check with your dentist first if you have gum disease under active treatment, recent oral surgery, or a newly placed crown or implant. Not intended for young children.
