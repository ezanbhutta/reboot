Technique

Three things decide whether this actually works for you.

Not pressure. Aim.

Step 01

Start on Soft

Fill the tank with clean, lukewarm water and tap the mode button until you are on Soft. It ramps up over three seconds rather than hitting full pressure at once, and it remembers where you left it.

Step 02

Aim where the tooth meets the gum

Hold the nozzle at roughly ninety degrees and trace the line where the tooth meets the gum. Pause briefly at each one.

Step 03

Lean over the sink, lips nearly closed

Let the water fall out as you go. Forty seconds, no mess.

Your first night

Charge it, then five steps.

Charge for two to three hours before the first use. The light blinks while it charges and turns solid green when it is full. After that, one charge lasts about seventy minutes of running on Soft.

01

Fill the tank

Clean, lukewarm water. Not hot — the tank is not built for it.

02

Fit a nozzle

Push it into the top until you hear it click, then turn it to point where you want it.

03

Lean over the sink

Tip in your mouth, lips a little closed, so the water runs back into the basin rather than the mirror.

04

Turn it on and move slowly

Along the gumline, pausing for a moment between each tooth. It cuts out on its own after about two minutes.

05

Switch off, empty the tank

Every time. Standing water in the tank is the one thing that shortens the life of one of these.

Light gum bleeding in the first one to two weeks is normal for a lot of people and usually stops as the gums settle. Begin on Soft and build up slowly.

Control

Four modes, and the one to start on.

Massage, Soft, Normal and Clean, in the order the mode button steps through them. Drag the rail or use the arrow keys. Pressure runs from 40 to 120 PSI, pulsing 1600 times a minute, and the flosser starts in whichever mode you left it in.

Care and safety

What the manual says, before you own it.

Printed on the sheet in the box. It is here too, because the last two points are worth knowing before you spend the money rather than after.

Changing a nozzle

  • To remove: press the release button and pull the nozzle out.
  • To fit: push a new one in until it clicks.
  • Replace a tip when it deforms or the spray stops running straight, roughly every three to six months.

Cleaning it

  • Turn the power off and empty the tank after every use.
  • Hold the body in one hand and the tank in the other, rotate counter‑clockwise, then pull down to detach it.
  • The whole body is IPX7, so it is safe to rinse under the tap. Wipe it with a soft damp cloth.
  • No strong cleaners, and nothing abrasive.

Safety

  • Do not use it with an open wound in your mouth or on your tongue.
  • Do not direct the jet under the tongue, or into the ears or nose.
  • Children under six need an adult supervising.
  • Do not fill the tank with very hot water.
  • Charge only with the cable that came in the box, and check the cable for damage before the first use and from time to time after.
  • Keep it away from fire and heated surfaces. Do not put the charger in water or handle it with wet hands.
  • Stop using it and contact us if it looks damaged or has been dropped.

The full list is in the manual. If you have gum disease under active treatment, recent oral surgery or a newly placed crown or implant, ask your dentist before you start.

Questions

Before you decide.

Will I see bits of food come out?

Sometimes, sometimes not. That is not a sign it is failing. Most of what it removes is soft plaque at the gumline, which you will not see. Judge it by how your gums look after two weeks.

Can I stop using string floss?

We would rather you did not. They do different jobs. If you genuinely will not floss, this is far better than nothing, but it is not a straight swap.

My gums bleed. Should I stop?

Usually not. It is generally existing inflammation rather than damage, and it typically settles within one to two weeks. Start low. If it has not improved after a fortnight, speak to your dentist.

Is it messy?

Only if you stand upright with your mouth open. Lean over the sink, lips almost closed. Everyone gets it within a couple of uses.

What if I do not get on with it?

Thirty days from delivery, we pay return postage, and you get a full refund. Use it properly for a few weeks first. That is what the window is for.

How long does a charge last?

About seventy minutes of running on Soft, from a two to three hour charge over USB‑C. At forty seconds a night that is most of two months. The light blinks while it charges and goes solid green when it is full.

Where do the spare nozzles live?

Inside the tank. It comes off the body with a counter‑clockwise twist, and the manual is explicit that it is meant to store the tips you are not using. No separate caddy to lose.

Can I rinse the whole thing?

Yes. The body is IPX7, so it can go under the tap and be wiped with a damp cloth. Keep strong cleaners away from it, and keep the charging cable dry.

Who should not use one?

Check with your dentist first if you have gum disease under active treatment, recent oral surgery, or a newly placed crown or implant. Not intended for young children.
