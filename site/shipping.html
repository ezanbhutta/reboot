Policies

Shipping

Free on every order. Dispatched within one working day.

The short version: shipping is free, orders leave within one working day, and delivery takes two to four working days. Nothing is added at checkout.

What it costs

Standard delivery is free on all orders. There is no minimum spend and no handling fee. The price you see on the product page is the price you pay.

When it ships

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after that, or at a weekend or public holiday, go out on the next working day.

You'll get a confirmation email when you order, and a second email with tracking when the parcel leaves us.

How long it takes

Two to four working days from dispatch for standard delivery. Remote areas can take an extra day or two.

Where we ship

Shipping destinations are being confirmed ahead of launch and will be listed here in full. If your country isn't covered at launch, the newsletter is the fastest way to hear when it is.

If something goes wrong

If tracking hasn't updated for three working days, or the parcel is marked delivered and isn't with you, contact us. We'll chase the carrier. That is our job, not yours. If it's genuinely lost we replace it.
